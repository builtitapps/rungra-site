# Rungra support

Rungra tracks calisthenics progressions and zone 2 cardio. Everything you log stays on your phone: there's no account and no Rungra server.

**Need help?** Email [builtitapps+rungra@gmail.com](mailto:builtitapps+rungra@gmail.com). Tell us your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Getting started

### Do I need an account?

No. Rungra works without one, and without an internet connection. Only purchases need a connection.

### Where is my training kept?

On your phone, in Rungra's own storage. To keep a copy somewhere else, export it (Settings → Your data → Export your data) or turn on automatic backup (Settings → Your data → Automatic backup, with Rungra Pro).

## Moving to a new phone

1. Install Rungra on the new phone.
2. On the welcome screen, tap **Restore your training**.
3. Choose a backup from the list. If you had automatic backup on, it's there. Otherwise tap **Choose a file** and pick an export from your old phone.

Restoring is free, with or without Pro.

## Apple Health and Health Connect

### Connecting

Go to Settings → Apple Health (on iPhone) or Settings → Health Connect (on Android), then tap **Connect**. Rungra reads your workouts and heart rate, and saves your strength sessions as workouts.

### A workout isn't showing, or it looks short

Your watch can take a few minutes to send a workout's heart rate to your phone. To sync again, pull down on the Today screen, or go to Settings → Apple Health (or Health Connect) → **Sync now**.

### My watch says more zone 2 minutes than Rungra does

Open the session in History and look at **Time in each zone**:

- **Most of the missing time is in zone 1 or zone 3:** Rungra's zone 2 range is different from your watch's. You can set your own range in Settings → Zones.
- **It says "No heart-rate reading for … min":** your watch didn't record heart rate for that part of the session, so that time can't be counted in any zone.

### Changing or removing access

- **iPhone:** Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Rungra.
- **Android:** open the Health Connect app → App permissions → Rungra.

## Purchases

### Restoring a purchase

Settings → **Restore purchases**. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

### Lifetime and subscriptions

Lifetime is a single payment. Annual and monthly plans renew until you cancel:

- **iPhone:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rungra.
- **Android:** Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Rungra.

### Refunds

Apple and Google handle payments, so refunds go through them: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) for the App Store, and Google Play's refund page for Android.

## Reminders

If reminders don't appear, check that notifications are allowed for Rungra in your phone's settings, then check Settings → Reminders in Rungra.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → **Delete all data** removes everything Rungra keeps on your phone. Deleting the app does the same. Workouts saved to Apple Health or Health Connect, and backups in your iCloud Drive or Google Drive, stay until you remove them there. The [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) explains how.

## A note on training

Rungra tracks your training. It isn't medical advice. Talk to a doctor or another qualified professional before you start a new exercise programme, especially if you have a health condition.
