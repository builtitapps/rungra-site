# Delete your Rungra data

This page is for **Rungra: Workout Tracker**, on the App Store and Google Play. Rungra has no accounts and no server. Nearly everything it keeps is on your phone, where you can delete it yourself. The one thing kept anywhere else is a record of a purchase, which we delete when you ask.

## On your phone

Settings → Your data → **Delete all data** removes every session, setting and ladder placement Rungra keeps on your phone, straight away. Deleting the app does the same.

## Your backups

Automatic backups are in your own storage, and only you can remove them:

- **iPhone (iCloud Drive):** Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Rungra.
- **Android (Google Drive):** on the web, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Rungra → Options → **Delete hidden app data**.

Workouts Rungra saved to Apple Health or Health Connect stay there until you remove them there.

## Purchase records: ask us

If you bought Rungra Pro, our purchase service (RevenueCat) keeps a record of the purchase, linked to an anonymous ID, so the purchase can be restored. It has no name or email. To have it deleted:

1. Email [builtitapps+rungra@gmail.com](mailto:builtitapps+rungra@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20data) with the subject **Delete my data**.
2. Include the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt email, and the date and plan you bought, so we can find the record.
3. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

**What stays:** Apple and Google keep their own records of the purchase, under their own policies. Your purchase itself isn't affected: **Restore purchases** in Rungra brings Pro back from the store at any time, which creates a new record.

More in the [privacy policy](https://builtitapps.github.io/rungra-site/privacy-policy).
